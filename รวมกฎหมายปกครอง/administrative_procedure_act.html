Section 1
Title of the Act · ชื่อพระราชบัญญัติ
🇹🇭 ม.1

Section 1. This Act is called the “Administrative Procedure Act, B.E. 2539”.

Section 2
Date of Enforcement · วันใช้บังคับพระราชบัญญัติ
🇹🇭 ม.2

Section 2.[1] This Act shall come into force upon the expiration of one hundred and eighty days as from the day following the date of its publication in the Government Gazette.

Note:
[1] Published in the Government Gazette, Vol. 113, Part 60a, dated 14th November B.E. 2539 (1996).
Section 3
Scope of Application and Minimum Standards · ขอบเขตการใช้บังคับและการยกเว้นกฎหมายที่มีมาตรฐานสูงกว่า
🇹🇭 ม.3

Section 3. Administrative procedures under various laws shall be in accordance with those prescribed in this Act, except in the case where any law prescribes a specific administrative procedure for any particular matter and contains rules guaranteeing fairness or standards in the performance of official duties not lower than the rules prescribed in this Act.

The provisions of paragraph one shall not apply to processes and periods of time for appeal or objection prescribed in the law.

Section 4
Exemptions from Application of the Act · ข้อยกเว้นการไม่ใช้บังคับพระราชบัญญัตินี้แก่หน่วยงานหรือกิจการเฉพาะ
🇹🇭 ม.4

Section 4. This Act shall not apply to:

(1) the National Assembly and the Council of Ministers;

(2) organs exercising powers specifically under the Constitution;

(3) consideration of the Prime Minister or a Minister in direct policy matters;

(4) trial and adjudication of cases by a Court and the performance of work of officials in court proceedings, execution of judgments, and deposit of property;

(5) consideration and adjudication of petitions and the giving of directions under the law on the Council of State;

(6) operations in connection with foreign policy;

(7) operations in connection with military service or officials performing tactical duties jointly with the military in the defence and maintenance of security of the Kingdom against both external and internal threats;

(8) operations under the criminal justice process;

(9) carrying out of affairs of religious organizations.

Any exemption from the application of the provisions of this Act to the carrying out of any activity or to any agency, other than those prescribed in paragraph one, shall be enacted as a Royal Decree upon the proposal of the Administrative Procedure Commission.

Section 5
Definitions · บทนิยามคำสำคัญในพระราชบัญญัติ
🇹🇭 ม.5

Section 5. In this Act:

“administrative procedure” means preparation and proceedings by an official in order to issue an administrative order or a by-law, and shall include any proceedings in administrative matters under this Act;

“administrative consideration” means preparation and proceedings by an official in order to issue an administrative order;

“administrative order” means:

(1) an exercise of power under the law by an official with the effect of creating a legal relationship between persons in such a way as to create, modify, transfer, preserve, extinguish, or produce an effect on the status of rights or duties of a person, whether permanently or temporarily, such as giving an order, granting permission, giving approval, deciding an appeal, certifying, and accepting registration, but shall not include the issuance of a by-law;

(2) other acts as prescribed in the ministerial regulation;

“by-law” means a Royal Decree, ministerial regulation, notification of a ministry, ordinance of local administration, rule, regulation, or any other provisions of general application without addressing any specific case or person;

“quasi-judicial commission” means a commission established under the law which provides organization and procedure for the adjudication of rights and duties under the law;

“official” means a person, group of persons, or juristic person exercising, or entrusted to exercise, the administrative power of the State in carrying out any particular act under the law, whether established within the bureaucratic system, a State enterprise, or other State undertaking or not;

“party” means an applicant or a person opposing an application, a person subject or likely to be subject to an administrative order, and a person who has intervened in the administrative proceedings by reason that his or her rights will be affected by the outcome of the administrative order.

Section 6
Charge and Control of the Act · ผู้รักษาการและอำนาจการออกกฎกระทรวงและประกาศ
🇹🇭 ม.6

Section 6. The Prime Minister shall have charge and control of the execution of this Act and shall have the power to issue ministerial regulations and notifications for the execution of this Act.

Such ministerial regulations and notifications shall come into force upon their publication in the Government Gazette.

Chapter I: Administrative Procedure Commission (Sec. 7–11) หมวด 1 คณะกรรมการวิธีปฏิบัติราชการทางปกครอง (ม.7–11)
5 Sections ▼
Section 7
Establishment and Composition of the Commission · การจัดตั้งและองค์ประกอบของคณะกรรมการวิธีปฏิบัติราชการทางปกครอง
🇹🇭 ม.7

Section 7. There shall be a commission called the “Administrative Procedure Commission” consisting of a Chairman, the Permanent Secretary of the Office of the Prime Minister, the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Interior, the Secretary-General to the Cabinet, the Secretary-General of the Civil Service Commission, the Secretary-General of the Council of State, and not less than five but not more than nine qualified members as members.

The Council of Ministers shall appoint the Chairman and qualified members from persons qualified in the field of Law, Public Administration, Political Science, Social Science, or the Administration of State Affairs, provided that such persons must not be persons holding a political position.

The Secretary-General of the Council of State shall appoint government officials of the Office of the Council of State as secretary and assistant secretaries.

Section 8
Term of Office of Appointed Members · วาระการดำรงตำแหน่งและการแต่งตั้งกรรมการผู้ทรงคุณวุฒิ
🇹🇭 ม.8

Section 8. Members appointed by the Council of Ministers shall hold office for a term of three years. A member who vacates office may be re-appointed.

In the case where a member vacates office at the expiration of term, if a new member has not yet been appointed, such member shall remain in office and continue the performance of duties until a new member has been appointed.

Section 9
Vacation of Office Prior to Expiration of Term · เหตุพ้นจากตำแหน่งก่อนครบวาระของกรรมการผู้ทรงคุณวุฒิ
🇹🇭 ม.9

Section 9. In addition to vacating office at the expiration of term under Section 8, a member appointed by the Council of Ministers vacates office upon a resolution of the Council of Ministers removing him or her from office or upon any ground under Section 76.

Section 10
Secretariat of the Commission · สำนักงานเลขานุการของคณะกรรมการวิธีปฏิบัติราชการทางปกครอง
🇹🇭 ม.10

Section 10. The Office of the Council of State shall perform duties as the secretariat of the Administrative Procedure Commission, being responsible for administrative work, meetings, study and compilation of information, and various activities in connection with the work of the Administrative Procedure Commission.

Section 11
Powers and Duties of the Commission · อำนาจหน้าที่ของคณะกรรมการวิธีปฏิบัติราชการทางปกครอง
🇹🇭 ม.11

Section 11. The Administrative Procedure Commission shall have the powers and duties as follows:

(1) to monitor, supervise, and provide advice in connection with the performance of work of officials in compliance with this Act;

(2) to give consultation to officials in connection with compliance with this Act as requested by such persons, in accordance with the rules prescribed by the Administrative Procedure Commission;

(3) to issue a written summons requiring an official or any other person to give explanations or opinions for supplementing the consideration;

(4) to make recommendations on the enactment of Royal Decrees and the issuance of ministerial regulations or notifications under this Act;

(5) to prepare reports in connection with compliance with this Act for submission to the Council of Ministers from time to time as is appropriate, but at least once a year, for the purpose of developing and improving the performance of administrative procedure to ensure greater fairness and efficiency;

(6) to carry out other matters as entrusted by the Council of Ministers or the Prime Minister.

### Part 1: Officials

Chapter II · Part 1: Competent Official (Sec. 12–20) หมวด 2 คำสั่งทางปกครอง · ส่วนที่ 1 เจ้าหน้าที่ (ม.12–20)
9 Sections ▼
Section 12
Competence of Officials · หลักอำนาจหน้าที่ของเจ้าหน้าที่ในการทำคำสั่งทางปกครอง
🇹🇭 ม.12

Section 12. An administrative order must be made by an official who has powers and duties in respect of that matter.

Section 13
Grounds for Disqualification Due to Conflict of Interest · เหตุห้ามเจ้าหน้าที่กระทำการพิจารณาทางปกครองเนื่องจากมีส่วนได้เสีย
🇹🇭 ม.13

Section 13. The following officials shall not carry out an administrative consideration:

(1) being a party oneself;

(2) being a fiancé, fiancée, or spouse of a party;

(3) being a relative of a party, viz., an ascendant or descendant of any degree, or a sibling or cousin within the third degree, or a relative by affinity within the second degree;

(4) being or having been a legal representative, curator, representative, or agent of a party;

(5) being a creditor or debtor, or an employer of a party;

(6) other cases as prescribed in the ministerial regulation.

Section 14
Procedure Upon Disqualification or Challenge of an Official · ขั้นตอนและหน้าที่เมื่อเจ้าหน้าที่ต้องหยุดการพิจารณาทางปกครอง
🇹🇭 ม.14

Section 14. Where there is a circumstance under Section 13 or a party challenges that any official is a person under Section 13, such official shall stay the consideration of the matter and notify his or her immediate superior in order that the said superior may issue a further order.

The submission of a challenge, consideration of a challenge, and issuance of an order instructing another official to take over the duties in place of the challenged person shall be in accordance with the rules and procedure prescribed in the ministerial regulation.

Section 15
Disqualification or Challenge of a Commission Member · การคัดค้านและการพิจารณาเหตุห้ามของกรรมการในคณะกรรมการที่มีอำนาจพิจารณา
🇹🇭 ม.15

Section 15. Where there is a circumstance under Section 13 or a party challenges that a member of any commission having the power to carry out an administrative consideration possesses such characteristic, the Chairman of the commission shall convene a meeting of the commission to consider the ground for challenge. At such meeting, the challenged member, after having explained the facts and answered questions, shall leave the meeting.

If any commission having the power to carry out an administrative consideration has a member who is challenged, during the time the challenged member has to leave the meeting, it shall be deemed that such commission consists of all members who are not challenged.

If the meeting passes a resolution allowing the challenged member to continue the performance of duties with the votes of not less than two-thirds of the unchallenged members, such member may continue the performance of duties. Such resolution shall be made by secret ballot and shall be final.

The submission of a challenge and consideration of a challenge shall be in accordance with the rules and procedure prescribed in the ministerial regulation.

Section 16
Challenge on Grounds of Bias or Lack of Impartiality · การคัดค้านเจ้าหน้าที่หรือกรรมการเนื่องจากมีสภาพร้ายแรงอันอาจทำให้การพิจารณาไม่เป็นกลาง
🇹🇭 ม.16

Section 16. In the case where there is any ground other than those provided in Section 13 concerning an official or a member of a commission having the power to carry out an administrative consideration which is of such a serious nature as may impair the impartiality of the administrative consideration, such official or member shall not carry out the administrative consideration in that matter.

In the case under paragraph one, proceedings shall be taken as follows:

(1) if such person is of the opinion on his or her own motion that he or she is subject to such ground, such person shall stay the consideration of the matter and notify his or her immediate superior or the Chairman of the commission, as the case may be;

(2) if a party challenges that such person is subject to such ground, and if such person is of the opinion that the ground as challenged does not exist, such person may continue to consider the matter, provided that he or she must notify his or her immediate superior or the Chairman of the commission, as the case may be;

(3) the superior of such person or the commission having the power to carry out the administrative consideration of which such person is a member shall, without delay, issue an order or pass a resolution, as the case may be, as to whether such person has competence to carry out the administrative consideration in that matter.

The provisions of Section 14 paragraph two and Section 15 paragraph two, paragraph three, and paragraph four shall apply mutatis mutandis.

Section 17
Validity of Prior Acts Before Stay of Consideration · ผลของการกระทำของเจ้าหน้าที่หรือกรรมการก่อนหยุดการพิจารณา
🇹🇭 ม.17

Section 17. Any acts of an official or a member of a commission having the power to carry out an administrative consideration which have been done prior to staying the consideration under Section 14 and Section 16 shall not be invalidated, unless the official taking over the duties in place of the challenged person or the commission having the power to carry out the administrative consideration, as the case may be, thinks it appropriate to reconduct any part of the proceedings.

Section 18
Exception for Compelling Urgency or Necessity · ข้อยกเว้นในกรณีมีความจำเป็นเร่งด่วนหรือไม่มีเจ้าหน้าที่อื่นปฏิบัติหน้าที่แทนได้
🇹🇭 ม.18

Section 18. The provisions of Section 13 to Section 16 shall not apply to a case of urgent necessity where a delay will cause detriment to the public interest or irreparable injury to the rights of a person, or where there is no other official to perform duties in place of such person.

Section 19
Acts of Official or Member Whose Appointment is Defective · ผลของการปฏิบัติหน้าที่ของเจ้าหน้าที่หรือกรรมการที่ขาดคุณสมบัติหรือแต่งตั้งไม่ชอบด้วยกฎหมาย
🇹🇭 ม.19

Section 19. If it subsequently appears that any official or member of a commission having the power to carry out an administrative consideration lacks qualifications or is under any prohibition or his or her appointment is unlawful, thereby causing such person to vacate office, such vacation of office shall not affect any act which such person has performed within his or her powers and duties.

Section 20
Meaning of Immediate Superior · ความหมายของผู้บังคับบัญชาเหนือตนขึ้นไปชั้นหนึ่ง
🇹🇭 ม.20

Section 20. An immediate superior under Section 14 and Section 16 shall include a person prescribed by the law to have the power of supervision or superintendence in the case of an official who has no direct superior, and the Prime Minister in the case where such official is a Minister.

### Part 2: Parties

Chapter II · Part 2: Parties (Sec. 21–25) หมวด 2 คำสั่งทางปกครอง · ส่วนที่ 2 คู่กรณี (ม.21–25)
5 Sections ▼
Section 21
Eligibility to Be a Party · ผู้ซึ่งอาจเป็นคู่กรณีในการพิจารณาทางปกครอง
🇹🇭 ม.21

Section 21. A natural person, group of persons, or juristic person may be a party to an administrative consideration within the scope in which his, her, or its rights are affected or may inevitably be affected.

Section 22
Capacity to Act in Administrative Proceedings · ความสามารถในการดำเนินกระบวนพิจารณาทางปกครอง
🇹🇭 ม.22

Section 22. A person having capacity to act in administrative proceedings must be:

(1) a person who is sui juris;

(2) a person prescribed by a specific provision of law to have capacity to act in a specified matter, even though such person is not yet sui juris or his or her capacity is restricted under the Civil and Commercial Code;

(3) a juristic person or group of persons under Section 21, through its representative or agent, as the case may be;

(4) a person prescribed by a Notification of the Prime Minister or of a person entrusted by the Prime Minister published in the Government Gazette to have capacity to act in a specified matter, even though such person is not yet sui juris or his or her capacity is restricted under the Civil and Commercial Code.

Section 23
Right to Bring a Lawyer or Advisor · สิทธินำทนายความหรือที่ปรึกษาเข้ามาในการพิจารณาทางปกครอง
🇹🇭 ม.23

Section 23. In an administrative consideration where a party must appear in person before an official, the party has the right to bring his or her lawyer or advisor into the administrative consideration.

Any act done by the lawyer or advisor in the presence of the party shall be deemed as the act of the party, unless the party objects at that time.

Section 24
Appointment of Representative · การแต่งตั้งผู้แทนให้กระทำการแทนในกระบวนการพิจารณาทางปกครอง
🇹🇭 ม.24

Section 24. A party may appoint in writing any person who is sui juris to carry out any specified act on his or her behalf in any administrative proceedings. In this instance, the official may conduct administrative proceedings with the party in person only where it is a matter in which such person has a direct duty to perform the act personally, and must also notify the person appointed to act on his or her behalf.

If it appears that any person appointed to act on behalf of a party does not have sufficient knowledge of the facts in that matter or there is a reason not to trust the competence of such person, the official shall notify the party without delay.

An appointment to act on behalf of a party shall not be deemed terminated on account of the death of the party or a change in the capacity or representation of the party, unless the successor by law of the party or the party revokes such appointment.

Section 25
Joint Representative in Mass Applications · ตัวแทนร่วมในกรณีที่มีผู้ยื่นคำขอหรือคู่กรณีเกินห้าสิบคน
🇹🇭 ม.25

Section 25. In the case where an application is submitted with more than fifty joint signatories or where more than fifty parties submit applications with identical or similar statements, if the application specifies any person as the representative of such persons or contains statements impliedly understood to that effect, the person so named shall be deemed as the joint representative of those parties.

In the case where more than fifty parties submit applications for an administrative order in the same matter without designating any person as their joint representative under paragraph one, the official in that matter shall appoint a person approved by the majority of the parties as the joint representative of such persons. In this case, Section 24 paragraph two and paragraph three shall apply mutatis mutandis.

A joint representative under paragraph one or paragraph two must be a natural person.

A party may revoke the authorization for the joint representative to act on his or her behalf at any time, provided that the party must give written notice to the official and proceed with any acts in the administrative proceedings on his or her own motion thereafter.

A joint representative may terminate his or her representation at any time, provided that he or she must give written notice to the official and must also notify every party thereof.

### Part 3: Consideration

Chapter II · Part 3: Consideration Procedure (Sec. 26–33) หมวด 2 คำสั่งทางปกครอง · ส่วนที่ 3 การพิจารณา (ม.26–33)
8 Sections ▼
Section 26
Language of Documents and Translation · ภาษาของเอกสารที่ยื่นต่อเจ้าหน้าที่และการทำคำแปลภาษาไทย
🇹🇭 ม.26

Section 26. Documents submitted to an official shall be drawn up in the Thai language. If a document is drawn up in a foreign language, the party shall prepare a Thai translation certified as accurate within the period of time specified by the official. In this case, such document shall be deemed to have been submitted to the official on the date the official receives such translation, unless the official accepts the document drawn up in the foreign language, in which case the date of submission of the document drawn up in the foreign language shall be deemed as the date on which the official receives such document.

The certification of accuracy of a Thai translation or the acceptance of a document drawn up in a foreign language shall be in accordance with the rules and procedure prescribed in the ministerial regulation.

Section 27
Notification of Rights and Verification of Applications · หน้าที่ของเจ้าหน้าที่ในการแจ้งสิทธิและตรวจสอบความถูกต้องครบถ้วนของคำขอและเอกสาร
🇹🇭 ม.27

Section 27.[2] An official shall notify the parties of their rights and duties in the administrative proceedings as is necessary for the case.

When a person submits an application for an official to issue an administrative order, it shall be the duty of the official receiving the application to examine the correctness of the application and the completeness of all documents required by the law or by-law to be submitted together with the application. If the application is not correct, such official shall give advice to the applicant to make corrections or amendments properly. If any document is incomplete, the official shall notify the applicant immediately or within not more than seven days as from the date of receipt of the application. In giving such notice, the official shall make it in writing signed by the receiver of the application and specify the list of documents that are incorrect or incomplete for the applicant’s information, and shall also record such notification in the proceedings for issuing that administrative order.

When the applicant has corrected the application or furnished the documents as specified in the notification under paragraph two completely, the official shall not refuse to proceed with the application by reason that other documents are still lacking, except where it is necessary for the purpose of complying with the law or by-law and approval has been obtained from the immediate superior under Section 20. In such case, the said superior shall inquire into the facts forthwith, and if he or she is of the opinion that it is due to a fault of the official, disciplinary action shall be taken accordingly.

The applicant must make corrections or furnish additional documents to the official within the time specified by the official or within the time extended by permission of the official. Upon the expiration of the said period of time, if the applicant fails to make corrections or furnish additional documents completely, the applicant shall be deemed as not wishing the official to proceed further with the application. In such case, the official shall return the documents to the applicant together with a notification of the right to appeal, and shall record such proceedings.

Note:
[2] Section 27 is amended by Section 3 of the Administrative Procedure Act (No. 2), B.E. 2557 (2014).
Section 28
Inquisitorial Fact-Finding Powers · อำนาจตรวจสอบข้อเท็จจริงของเจ้าหน้าที่ตามระบบไต่สวน
🇹🇭 ม.28

Section 28. In an administrative consideration, an official may examine and inquire into the facts as is appropriate to the matter without being bound by the applications or evidence of the parties.

Section 29
Consideration and Gathering of Evidence · หลักการพิจารณาและแสวงหาพยานหลักฐานในการพิสูจน์ข้อเท็จจริง
🇹🇭 ม.29

Section 29. An official must consider the evidence which he or she considers necessary for proving the facts. For this purpose, this shall include taking the following actions:

(1) seeking all relevant evidence;

(2) hearing evidence, explanations, or opinions of the parties or of witnesses or expert witnesses adduced by the parties, unless the official is of the opinion that it is an unnecessary or superfluous allegation or is intended to prolong the proceedings;

(3) requesting facts or opinions from the parties, witnesses, or expert witnesses;

(4) requesting possessors of documents to furnish relevant documents;

(5) examining a place on site.

The parties must cooperate with the official in proving the facts and have the duty to inform the official of the evidence known to them.

Witnesses or expert witnesses summoned by the official to provide statements or opinions shall be entitled to remuneration in accordance with the rules and procedure prescribed in the ministerial regulation.

Section 30
Right of the Parties to Be Heard · สิทธิของคู่กรณีในการได้รับแจ้งข้อเท็จจริงและโต้แย้งพยานหลักฐาน
🇹🇭 ม.30

Section 30. In the case where an administrative order may affect the rights of a party, the official shall afford the party an opportunity to be informed of the facts sufficiently and an opportunity to contest and present his or her evidence.

The provisions of paragraph one shall not apply in the following cases, unless the official thinks it appropriate to act otherwise:

(1) where there is compelling urgency such that a delay will cause serious injury to any person or will affect the public interest;

(2) where it will have the effect of delaying the period of time prescribed by the law or by-law for making the administrative order;

(3) where they are facts which the party himself or herself has already given in an application, statement, or explanation;

(4) where, by the nature of the matter, it is self-evident that affording such opportunity cannot be done;

(5) where it is a measure of administrative enforcement;

(6) other cases as prescribed in the ministerial regulation.

The official shall be prohibited from affording the opportunity under paragraph one if it will cause serious detriment to the public interest.

Section 31
Right of the Parties to Examine Documents · สิทธิของคู่กรณีในการขอตรวจดูเอกสารที่จำเป็นแก่การโต้แย้ง
🇹🇭 ม.31

Section 31. A party has the right to request to examine documents that are necessary to know for contesting, giving explanations, or defending his or her rights; however, if an administrative order has not yet been made in that matter, the party does not have the right to request to examine documents which are drafts of the decision.

The examination of documents, expenses in examining documents, or preparation of copies of documents shall be in accordance with the rules and procedure prescribed in the ministerial regulation.

Section 32. An official may not grant permission to examine documents or evidence if it is a case where confidentiality must be maintained.

Section 33

🇹🇭 ม.33

Section 33. In the case where the operation in any matter must pass through the consideration of more than one official, the officials concerned have the duty to co-ordinate with one another in specifying the timeframe for the operation in that matter.

### Part 4: Form and Effect of Administrative Orders

Chapter II · Part 4: Form and Effect of Administrative Orders (Sec. 34–43) หมวด 2 คำสั่งทางปกครอง · ส่วนที่ 4 รูปแบบและผลของคำสั่งทางปกครอง (ม.34–43)
11 Sections ▼
Section 34
Forms of Administrative Orders · รูปแบบของคำสั่งทางปกครอง
🇹🇭 ม.34

Section 34. An administrative order may be made in writing or orally or by conveying meaning in another form, provided that it must contain statements or meaning sufficiently clear to be understood.

Section 35
Written Confirmation of Oral Administrative Orders · คำสั่งทางปกครองด้วยวาจาและการขอให้ยืนยันเป็นหนังสือ
🇹🇭 ม.35

Section 35. In the case where an administrative order is an oral order, if the recipient of that order makes a request and the request is made with reasonable cause within seven days as from the date such order was given, the official issuing the order shall confirm that order in writing.

Section 36
Essential Contents of Written Administrative Orders · รายละเอียดและองค์ประกอบที่ต้องระบุในคำสั่งทางปกครองที่เป็นหนังสือ
🇹🇭 ม.36

Section 36. A written administrative order shall at least specify the day, month, and year in which the order is made, the name and position of the official making the order, and shall bear the signature of the official making that order.

Section 37
Requirement of Reasons in Written Administrative Orders · การระบุเหตุผลประกอบคำสั่งทางปกครองและข้อยกเว้น
🇹🇭 ม.37

Section 37. A written administrative order and a written confirmation of an administrative order must also be accompanied by reasons, and such reasons shall at least consist of:

(1) material facts;

(2) provisions of law relied upon;

(3) considerations and supporting grounds in the exercise of discretion.

The Prime Minister or a person entrusted by the Prime Minister may publish a Notification in the Government Gazette requiring that an administrative order in any particular case must specify the reasons in the order itself or in a document annexed to that order.

The provisions of paragraph one shall not apply to the following cases:

(1) where the result is in accordance with the application and does not affect the rights and duties of any other person;

(2) where the reasons are already well known without the need to specify them again;

(3) where it is a case in which confidentiality must be maintained under Section 32;

(4) where it is the issuance of an oral administrative order or a case of urgency, provided that reasons must be given in writing within a reasonable time if the person subject to that order so requests.

Section 38
Exemptions from Form and Reasoning Requirements · ข้อยกเว้นการไม่นำมาตรา 36 และมาตรา 37 วรรคหนึ่ง มาใช้บังคับตามกฎกระทรวง
🇹🇭 ม.38

Section 38. The provisions of Section 36 and Section 37 paragraph one shall not apply to administrative orders prescribed in the ministerial regulation, in accordance with the rules, procedure, and conditions prescribed in the ministerial regulation.

Section 39
Prescription of Conditions in Administrative Orders · การกำหนดเงื่อนไขในคำสั่งทางปกครอง
🇹🇭 ม.39

Section 39. In issuing an administrative order, an official may prescribe any conditions insofar as is necessary to achieve the objectives of the law, unless the law prescribes restrictions on the exercise of discretion otherwise.

The prescription of conditions under paragraph one shall include the prescription of conditions in the following cases, as is appropriate to the case:

(1) prescribing that a right or obligation shall take effect or cease to have effect at any particular time;

(2) prescribing that the taking effect or cessation of effect of a right or obligation shall depend upon an uncertain future event;

(3) a reservation of the right to revoke the administrative order;

(4) prescribing that the beneficiary must act or refrain from any act, or must have an obligation or accept a certain obligation or responsibility, or prescribing provisions for providing, modifying, or adding to such requirements.

Section 39/1
Time Limit for Issuing Written Administrative Orders · กำหนดเวลา 30 วันในการออกคำสั่งทางปกครองเป็นหนังสือ
🇹🇭 ม.39/1

Section 39/1.[3] In issuing a written administrative order in any matter, if no law or by-law prescribes a period of time for issuing an administrative order in that matter otherwise, the official shall complete the issuance of that administrative order within thirty days as from the date the official receives the application and correct and complete documents.

It shall be the duty of the superior of the official to supervise and ensure that the official acts in compliance with paragraph one.

Section 40
Specification of Procedure and Time-Limit for Appeal or Objection · การแจ้งสิทธิและระยะเวลาอุทธรณ์หรือโต้แย้งในคำสั่งทางปกครอง

In the case where there is a violation of the provisions of paragraph one, the time for the appeal or objection shall restart as from the date of receipt of notification of the rules under paragraph one; however, if no new notification is made and the time-limit thereof is less than one year, it shall be extended to a one-year period as from the date of the receipt of the administrative order.

Section 41
Rectification of Procedural or Formal Defects · การแก้ไขคำสั่งทางปกครองที่บกพร่องเรื่องรูปแบบและขั้นตอนให้สมบูรณ์
🇹🇭 ม.41

Section 41. An administrative order issued in violation of or non-compliance with the following rules shall not render that administrative order invalid:

(1) the issuance of an administrative order without an application having yet been submitted in a case where the official cannot act on his or her own initiative unless an application is submitted, if such an application is subsequently submitted;

(2) an administrative order which must be accompanied by reasons under Section 37 paragraph one, if such reasons are subsequently provided;

(3) the hearing of a party which is required to be carried out was conducted incompletely, if a complete hearing is subsequently conducted;

(4) an administrative order which requires the prior approval of another official, if that official subsequently gives such approval.

When action under paragraph one (1), (2), (3), or (4) has been taken and the official issuing the administrative order intends for the effect to be in accordance with the original order, such official shall record the facts and his or her intention in or annexed to the original order and must give written notice of his or her intention to the party.

Cases under (2), (3), and (4) must be carried out before the termination of the appeal proceedings under Part 5 of this Chapter or under the specific law governing that matter, or, if it is a case not subject to such appeal, before the administrative order is brought for consideration by a person having competence to consider and adjudicate the legality of that administrative order.

Section 42
Binding Effect and Termination of Administrative Orders · ผลบังคับของคำสั่งทางปกครองและการสิ้นผลบังคับ
🇹🇭 ม.42

Section 42. An administrative order shall take effect vis-à-vis a person as from the moment such person receives notification thereof.

An administrative order shall remain in effect so long as it has not been revoked or terminated by a time condition or by any other cause.

When an administrative order ceases to have effect, the official shall have the power to summon the person in possession of any document or other object drawn up or prepared in connection with the issuance of such administrative order, which contains statements or marks indicating the existence of that administrative order, to return that item or to bring such item belonging to that person to the official to make a mark indicating the termination of effect of such administrative order.

Section 43

🇹🇭 ม.43

Section 43. An administrative order containing minor errors or minor mistakes may be corrected or amended by the official at any time.

In correcting or amending an administrative order under paragraph one, the official shall notify the persons concerned as is appropriate to the case. For this purpose, the official may summon the persons concerned to furnish the administrative order, document, or any other object drawn up or prepared in connection with the issuance of such administrative order for making the correction or amendment.

### Part 5: Appeal Against Administrative Orders

Chapter II · Part 5: Appeal Against Administrative Orders (Sec. 44–48) หมวด 2 คำสั่งทางปกครอง · ส่วนที่ 5 การอุทธรณ์คำสั่งทางปกครอง (ม.44–48)

Section 44
Right and Time-Limit for Lodging an Administrative Appeal · สิทธิและระยะเวลาในการยื่นอุทธรณ์คำสั่งทางปกครอง
🇹🇭 ม.44

Section 44. Subject to Section 48, in the case where any administrative order is not issued by a Minister and there is no law specifically prescribing a process for appeal within the administration, a party may appeal against that administrative order by submitting the appeal to the official who made the administrative order within fifteen days as from the date on which he or she received notification of such order.

Section 45

Section 45. The official under Section 44 paragraph one shall consider the appeal and notify the appellant without delay, provided that it shall not exceed thirty days as from the date of receipt of the appeal. In the case where the official agrees with the appeal, whether in whole or in part, the official shall proceed to vary the administrative order in accordance with his or her opinion within the said period of time.

If the official under Section 44 paragraph one disagrees with the appeal, whether in whole or in part, the official shall expeditiously report his or her opinion together with reasons to the person having competence to consider the appeal within the period of time under paragraph one. The person having competence to consider the appeal shall complete the consideration within thirty days as from the date on which he or she receives the report. If there is a necessary cause preventing completion of the consideration within the said period of time, the person having competence to consider the appeal shall give written notice to the appellant before the expiration of the said period of time. In this instance, the period of time for considering the appeal may be extended for not more than thirty days as from the date of expiration of the said period of time.

Which official shall be the person having competence to consider an appeal under paragraph two shall be in accordance with that prescribed in the ministerial regulation.

The provisions of this Section shall not apply to cases where a specific law provides otherwise.

Chapter II · Part 6: Revocation of Administrative Orders (Sec. 49–53) หมวด 2 คำสั่งทางปกครอง · ส่วนที่ 6 การเพิกถอนคำสั่งทางปกครอง (ม.49–53)
5 Sections ▼
Section 49
General Competence and Time-Limit for Revocation · อำนาจและกำหนดเวลาในการเพิกถอนคำสั่งทางปกครอง
🇹🇭 ม.49

Section 49. An official or the superior of an official may revoke an administrative order in accordance with the rules in Section 51, Section 52, and Section 53, irrespective of whether the process prescribed for appeal or objection under this Act or other laws has elapsed or not.

The revocation of an administrative order of a beneficial nature must be made within ninety days as from the day the ground for revoking that administrative order is known, unless the administrative order was made by reason of making a false statement or concealing facts which should have been disclosed, or threat, or inducement by an unlawful giving of property or any other benefit.

Section 50
Revocation of Unlawful Administrative Orders · หลักเกณฑ์การเพิกถอนคำสั่งทางปกครองที่ไม่ชอบด้วยกฎหมาย
🇹🇭 ม.50

Section 50. An unlawful administrative order may be revoked, in whole or in part, by directing that its effect be retrospective or non-retrospective or prospective to any particular time as specified; however, if that order is an order granting a benefit to the recipient, the revocation must be in accordance with the provisions of Section 51 and Section 52.

Section 51
Revocation of Unlawful Administrative Orders Granting Money, Property, or Divisible Benefits · การเพิกถอนคำสั่งทางปกครองที่ไม่ชอบด้วยกฎหมายซึ่งให้เงิน ทรัพย์สิน หรือประโยชน์ที่อาจแบ่งแยกได้
🇹🇭 ม.51

Section 51. In revoking an unlawful administrative order which grants money or property or a benefit that is divisible, there shall be taken into consideration the reliance in good faith of the beneficiary on the continued existence of that administrative order together with the public interest.

Reliance in good faith under paragraph one shall be protected only where the recipient of the administrative order has utilized the benefit arising from the administrative order or has carried out acts in connection with property which cannot be rectified or altered, or where the alteration will cause undue injury to such person.

In the following cases, the recipient of the administrative order may not rely on good faith:

(1) such person made a false statement or concealed facts which should have been disclosed, or used threats, or induced the order by an unlawful giving of property or any other benefit;

(2) such person furnished statements which were incorrect or incomplete in a material respect;

(3) such person knew of the unlawfulness of the administrative order at the time of receiving the administrative order, or his or her lack of knowledge was due to gross negligence.

In the case where revocation is made with retrospective effect, the provisions on undue enrichment in the Civil and Commercial Code shall apply mutatis mutandis to the return of money, property, or benefits received by the recipient of the administrative order, provided that whenever the recipient of the administrative order has known of the unlawfulness of the administrative order or should have known thereof had such person not been grossly negligent, such person shall be deemed to be in bad faith as from that time onwards, and in the cases under paragraph three, such person shall be liable for returning the money, property, or benefits received in full.

Section 52
Revocation of Other Unlawful Beneficial Administrative Orders and Compensation · การเพิกถอนคำสั่งทางปกครองที่ไม่ชอบด้วยกฎหมายกรณีอื่นและสิทธิได้รับค่าทดแทนความเสียหาย
🇹🇭 ม.52

Section 52. An unlawful administrative order which is not subject to Section 51 may be revoked in whole or in part; however, a person affected by the revocation of such administrative order shall be entitled to receive compensation for injury resulting from reliance in good faith on the continued existence of the administrative order, and the provisions of Section 51 paragraph one, paragraph two, and paragraph three shall apply mutatis mutandis, provided that the request for compensation must be made within one hundred and eighty days as from the date of receiving notification of that revocation.

Compensation for injury under this Section must not be higher than the benefit which such person might have received had such administrative order not been revoked.

Section 53
Revocation of Lawful Administrative Orders · การเพิกถอนคำสั่งทางปกครองที่ชอบด้วยกฎหมาย
🇹🇭 ม.53

Section 53. A lawful administrative order which does not grant a benefit to the recipient of the administrative order may be revoked, in whole or in part, by directing that its effect be as from the time of revocation or be prospective to any particular time as specified, except where it is a case in which an administrative order with similar content would still have to be made again or where the revocation cannot be made on account of other grounds; provided that the interests of a third person shall also be taken into consideration.

A lawful administrative order which grants a benefit to the recipient of the administrative order may be revoked, in whole or in part, by directing that its effect be as from the time of revocation or be prospective to any particular time as specified, only in the following circumstances:

(1) where the law provides for revocation or there is a reservation of the right to revoke in the administrative order itself;

(2) where the administrative order contains a requirement with which the beneficiary must comply, but there has been no compliance within the specified time;

(3) where facts and circumstances have changed which, had such facts and circumstances existed at the time of making the administrative order, the official would not have made that administrative order, and without revocation detriment to the public interest will be caused;

(4) where a provision of law has changed which, had such provision of law existed at the time of making the administrative order, the official would not have made that administrative order, provided that revocation in this case may be made insofar as the beneficiary has not yet utilized the benefit or has not yet received the benefit under such administrative order, and without revocation detriment to the public interest will be caused;

(5) where serious detriment to the public interest or to the people may occur, making it necessary to prevent or eliminate such circumstance.

In the case where an administrative order is revoked by reason of the grounds under paragraph two (3), (4), and (5), the beneficiary shall be entitled to receive compensation for injury arising from reliance in good faith on the continued existence of the administrative order, and Section 52 shall apply mutatis mutandis.

A lawful administrative order which grants money or property or a benefit that is divisible may be revoked, in whole or in part, by directing that its effect be retrospective or non-retrospective or prospective to any particular time as specified in the following cases:

(1) failure to comply or delay in complying in order to carry out the objectives of the administrative order;

(2) the beneficiary fails to comply or delays in complying with the conditions of the administrative order.

For this purpose, the provisions of Section 51 shall apply mutatis mutandis.

Chapter III: Period of Time and Prescription (Sec. 64–67) หมวด 3 ระยะเวลาและอายุความ (ม.64–67)
4 Sections ▼
Section 64
Computation of Periods of Time · หลักเกณฑ์การนับระยะเวลาเป็นวัน สัปดาห์ เดือน หรือปี
🇹🇭 ม.64

Section 64. In computing a period of time in days, weeks, months, or years, the first day of that period of time shall not be reckoned therewith, unless the act has commenced on that day or it is prescribed otherwise by the official.

In the case where an official has a duty to perform any particular act within a specified period of time, the last day of that period of time shall be reckoned therewith even though the final day is a non-working day for the official.

In the case where any person must perform any particular act within a period of time prescribed by the law or by an order of an official, if the final day is a non-working day for the official or a traditional holiday of the recipient of the order, it shall be deemed that the period of time expires on the working day following that holiday, unless the law or the official issuing the order prescribes otherwise.

Section 65
Extension of Periods of Time Prescribed in Orders · อำนาจการขยายระยะเวลาที่กำหนดไว้ในคำสั่งของเจ้าหน้าที่
🇹🇭 ม.65

Section 65. A period of time prescribed in an order of an official may be extended further, and if that period of time has already expired, the official may also extend it by prescribing retrospective effect if its expiration according to the original period of time would cause unfairness.

Section 66
Application for Extension of Statutory Time-Limit Due to Necessary Circumstances · การขอขยายระยะเวลาตามกฎหมายเมื่อมีพฤติการณ์จำเป็นอันมิได้เกิดจากความผิดของผู้ยื่นคำขอ
🇹🇭 ม.66

Section 66. In the case where any person is unable to perform any particular act within a period of time prescribed in the law by reason of necessary circumstances not arising from the fault of that person, if such person submits an application, the official may extend the period of time and reconduct any part of the proceedings that has already elapsed; provided that the application must be submitted within fifteen days as from the day such circumstances ceased.

Section 67
Interruption of Prescription Upon Appeal or Submission to Quasi-Judicial Commission · การสะดุดหยุดอยู่ของอายุความเมื่อมีการอุทธรณ์หรือยื่นคำขอต่อคณะกรรมการวินิจฉัยข้อพิพาท
🇹🇭 ม.67

Section 67. When an appeal is made under the provisions of Part 5 of Chapter II of this Act, or an application is submitted to a quasi-judicial commission or the Petition Council under the law on the Council of State for adjudication, the period of prescription shall be interrupted and shall not be reckoned in the meantime until the consideration becomes final or is otherwise concluded; however, if it is concluded by reason of withdrawal of the application or abandonment of the application, the period of prescription of the applicant’s claim shall be deemed never to have been interrupted.

Chapter IV: Notification (Sec. 68–74) หมวด 4 การแจ้ง (ม.68–74)
7 Sections ▼
Section 68
Scope of Application of Notification Provisions · ข้อยกเว้นรูปแบบการแจ้งที่ไม่สามารถทำโดยวาจาหรือเป็นหนังสือได้
🇹🇭 ม.68

Section 68. The provisions of this Chapter shall not apply to a notification which cannot be made orally or in writing or where a law prescribes the procedure for notification otherwise.

In the case of an administrative order communicated by conveying meaning in another form as prescribed in the ministerial regulation, it shall take effect when notification has been made.

Section 69
Oral and Written Notification and Delivery to Domicile or Given Address · รูปแบบและช่องทางการแจ้งคำสั่งทางปกครองหรือการนัดพิจารณา
🇹🇭 ม.69

Section 69. Notification of an administrative order, an appointment for consideration, or any other act which an official must notify to a person concerned may be made orally; however, if that person wishes it to be made in writing, it shall be notified in writing.

A written notification shall be delivered to that person, or if it has been sent to the domicile of that person, it shall be deemed to have been received as from the moment it arrives.

In carrying out any matter where an address has already been given to the official, notification sent to such address shall be deemed as notification to the domicile of that person.

Section 70
Notification by Personal Delivery, Substitute Delivery, or Posting · การแจ้งเป็นหนังสือโดยวิธีส่งมอบ ณ ภูมิลำเนาและการวางหรือปิดหนังสือแจ้ง
🇹🇭 ม.70

Section 70. In making a written notification by way of personal delivery, if the recipient refuses to accept it, or if at the time of delivery the recipient is not found and it has been delivered to any person who is sui juris residing or working at that place, or in the case where such person refuses to accept it, if the notice has been left or posted in a conspicuous place at that place in the presence of an official as prescribed in the ministerial regulation accompanying as a witness, it shall be deemed that notification has been received.

Section 71
Notification by Registered Post with Return Receipt · การแจ้งโดยวิธีส่งทางไปรษณีย์ตอบรับและวันถือว่าได้รับแจ้ง
🇹🇭 ม.71

Section 71. Notification by way of registered post with return receipt shall be deemed to have been received upon the expiration of seven days as from the date of posting for domestic cases, or upon the expiration of fifteen days as from the date of posting for cases sent to a foreign country, unless it can be proved that there was no receipt or that it was received before or after that day.

Section 72
Notification to More Than Fifty Recipients by Posting Notice · วิธีการแจ้งคำสั่งทางปกครองแก่บุคคลจำนวนเกินห้าสิบคน
🇹🇭 ม.72

Section 72. In the case where there are more than fifty recipients, the official may give notice from the commencement of proceedings in that matter that notification to those persons will be made by way of posting a notice at the office of the official and at the District Office where the recipients are domiciled. In this case, it shall be deemed to have been received upon the lapse of a period of fifteen days as from the date notification was made by such method.

Section 73
Notification by Publication in Newspaper · วิธีการแจ้งโดยการประกาศในหนังสือพิมพ์กรณีไม่รู้ตัวผู้รับ ไม่รู้ภูมิลำเนา หรือมีผู้รับเกินหนึ่งร้อยคน
🇹🇭 ม.73

Section 73. In the case where the recipient is unknown, or is known but the domicile is unknown, or both the recipient and domicile are known but there are more than one hundred recipients, written notification may be made by publication in a newspaper widely circulated in that locality. In this case, it shall be deemed to have been received upon the lapse of a period of fifteen days as from the date notification was made by such method.

Section 74
Notification by Facsimile in Cases of Urgent Necessity · การแจ้งคำสั่งทางปกครองทางเครื่องโทรสารในกรณีจำเป็นเร่งด่วน
🇹🇭 ม.74

Section 74. In the case of urgent necessity, notification of an administrative order may be made by way of facsimile transmission, provided that there must be evidence of transmission from the telecommunications service agency acting as the medium for transmitting that facsimile, and the original administrative order must be sent by any procedure under this Chapter to the recipient as soon as practicable. In this case, the recipient shall be deemed to have received notification of the written administrative order as of the date and time appearing in the evidence of the said telecommunications service agency, unless it can be proved that there was no receipt or that it was received before or after that time.

Chapter V: Commissions Having Power to Conduct Administrative Procedure (Sec. 75–84) หมวด 5 คณะกรรมการที่มีอำนาจดำเนินการพิจารณาทางปกครอง (ม.75–84)

Section 75
Appointment of Qualified Members by Specifying the Individual · การแต่งตั้งกรรมการผู้ทรงคุณวุฒิโดยระบุตัวบุคคล
🇹🇭 ม.75

Section 75. The appointment of a member in the character of a qualified member shall be made by specifying the individual person.

Section 76
Vacation of Office of Commission Members · เหตุพ้นจากตำแหน่งก่อนวาระของกรรมการในคณะกรรมการ
🇹🇭 ม.76

Section 76. In addition to vacating office at the expiration of term, a member vacates office upon:

(1) death;

(2) resignation;

(3) being a bankrupt;

(4) being an incompetent or quasi-incompetent person;

(5) being imprisoned by a final judgment to a term of imprisonment, except for a petty offence or an offence committed through negligence;

(6) being subject to a ground requiring vacation of office before the expiration of term under the law governing that matter.

Section 77
Appointment of Replacing or Additional Commission Members · การแต่งตั้งกรรมการแทนตำแหน่งที่ว่างหรือแต่งตั้งเพิ่มขึ้นและวาระการดำรงตำแหน่ง
🇹🇭 ม.77

Section 77. In the case where a member vacates office before the expiration of term, the appointing authority may appoint another person as a replacing member, and the person appointed to replace the member shall hold office for the remaining term of the person replaced.

In the case where additional members are appointed while the members already appointed still have a term in office, the person appointed as an additional member shall hold office for the remaining term of the members already appointed.

Section 78
Protection Against Removal of Members of a Quasi-Judicial Commission · เงื่อนไขและเหตุในการให้กรรมการในคณะกรรมการวินิจฉัยข้อพิพาทพ้นจากตำแหน่งก่อนครบวาระ
🇹🇭 ม.78

Section 78. Subject to Section 76, the removal from office of a member of a quasi-judicial commission before the expiration of term shall not be permitted, except in the case of gross neglect in the performance of duties or grave misconduct.

Section 79
Quorum for Meetings of a Commission · องค์ประชุมของการประชุมคณะกรรมการและการประชุมซ้ำกรณีไม่ครบองค์ประชุม
🇹🇭 ม.79

Section 79. Subject to Section 15 paragraph two, in meetings of a commission, the presence of at least one-half of the members is required to constitute a quorum, unless the provisions of a law, by-law, or order establishing that commission provide otherwise.

In the case where there is a sufficient number of members to constitute a quorum, if the consideration of any matter must be adjourned for lack of a quorum, and if it is a meeting of a commission which is not a quasi-judicial commission, if another meeting on that matter is convened within fourteen days as from the adjourned meeting date and at this subsequent meeting not less than one-third of the total number of members attend, it shall be deemed to constitute a quorum; provided that the intention to bring about the effect under this provision must also be specified in the written notice convening the meeting.

Section 80
Rules of Procedure and Notice of Meetings · การนัดประชุมและระเบียบการประชุมของคณะกรรมการ
🇹🇭 ม.80

Section 80. Meetings shall be in accordance with the rules of procedure prescribed by the commission.

A notice convening a meeting must be made in writing and notified to every member at least three days in advance, unless that member has already been informed of the appointment at a meeting, in which case a written notice may be made only to the members who did not attend the meeting.

The provisions of paragraph two shall not apply in the case of urgent necessity, in which case the Chairman of the commission may convene a meeting otherwise.

Section 81
Powers and Duties of the Chairman at Meetings and Acting Chairman · อำนาจหน้าที่ของประธานกรรมการในการดำเนินการประชุมและการทำหน้าที่แทน
🇹🇭 ม.81

Section 81. The Chairman of the commission has the powers and duties to conduct the meeting, and in order to maintain order at the meeting, the Chairman shall have the power to issue any orders as is necessary.

If the Chairman of the commission is not present at the meeting or is unable to perform duties, the Vice Chairman shall perform the duties in his or her place. If there is no Vice Chairman or there is such person but the person is unable to perform the duties, the members present at the meeting shall elect one member to perform the duties in his or her place.

In the case where the Chairman of the commission has a duty to carry out any act other than conducting a meeting, the provisions of paragraph two shall apply mutatis mutandis.

Section 82
Resolutions, Voting, and Casting Vote at Meetings · มติที่ประชุม หลักเกณฑ์การลงคะแนน และเสียงชี้ขาดของประธาน
🇹🇭 ม.82

Section 82. A resolution of a meeting shall be made by a majority of votes.

Each member shall have one vote in voting. In the case of an equality of votes, the person presiding over the meeting shall have an additional vote as a casting vote.

In any matter where there is no person objecting, the person presiding shall ask the meeting whether any person is of a different opinion. When no person is of a different opinion, the meeting shall be deemed to have passed a resolution approving that matter.

Section 83
Written Minutes of Meetings and Recording of Dissenting Opinions · การจัดทำรายงานการประชุมและการบันทึกความเห็นแย้ง
🇹🇭 ม.83

Section 83. At a meeting, there must be written minutes of the meeting.

If there is a dissenting opinion, the dissenting opinion together with reasons shall be recorded in the minutes of the meeting, and if the minority members submit a dissenting opinion in writing, that dissenting opinion shall also be recorded.

Section 84
Signatures and Dissenting Opinions in Decisions of a Quasi-Judicial Commission · การลงลายมือชื่อและการทำความเห็นแย้งในคำวินิจฉัยของคณะกรรมการวินิจฉัยข้อพิพาท
🇹🇭 ม.84

Section 84. A decision of a quasi-judicial commission must bear the signatures of the members adjudicating that matter.

In the case where any member has a dissenting opinion, he or she shall have the right to write his or her dissenting opinion included in the decision.

Remarks
Reasons for Promulgation and Key Amending Acts · หมายเหตุและเหตุผลในการประกาศใช้

Countersigned by: Banharn Silpa-archa, Prime Minister.

Reasons for Promulgation of B.E. 2539 (1996):

Whereas the performance of administrative work at present frequently involves the exercise of power under the law against private individuals, whether by giving an order, granting permission, giving approval, or carrying out any other acts affecting the status of rights or duties of persons, it is appropriate to establish clear general standards and rules for administrative procedures so that the performance of official duties will be conducted with transparency, efficiency, public participation, rapid examination of facts, impartiality, and fairness to the parties concerned, thereby protecting the rights and liberties of the people and preventing arbitrary exercise of state power. It is therefore necessary to enact this Act.

Key Amending Acts:

  • Administrative Procedure Act (No. 2), B.E. 2557 (2014): Added Section 39/1 establishing clear timeframes (30 days plus one extension of up to 30 days) for an official to complete the consideration and issuance of an administrative order upon receiving a complete application.
  • Administrative Procedure Act (No. 3), B.E. 2562 (2019): Repealed the previous provisions on administrative enforcement (Sections 55–63) and replaced them with a comprehensive framework in Part 8 (Sections 63/1 to 63/25), divided into Division 1 (Seizure and Attachment of Property and Sale by Public Auction to Enforce Payment of Money, Sections 63/7–63/18) and Division 2 (Enforcement of Performance or Abstention from an Act, Sections 63/19–63/25), aligning administrative execution rules with the Civil Procedure Code and Administrative Court jurisdiction.
